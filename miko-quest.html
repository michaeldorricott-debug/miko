今日是你十三歲生日。多年來，我一直在等待寫下這封信的時刻 — 因為我始終知道，這個清晨終將來臨。

當太陽升起香港之上，這座城市對其他人來說與往常無異 — 天星小輪依舊穿越維港，茶樓裡人聲鼎沸，麻瓜們各自忙碌。然而在這片燈火之下，我們最古老的陰影已再度甦醒。

這一次，他沒有回到英倫。他選擇了這裡 — 一座他以為光明早已遺忘的城市。

而他做了我多年來最害怕他會做的事。

他擄走了 Harry。他擄走了 Ron。他擄走了 Hermione。

那三位曾在不列顛森林中與他對峙之人，如今被囚於這座海港燈火之下的某處。他封印了他們的魔法，使他們無法觸及他。能潛入他藏身之處的，唯有他不知姓名之人 — 那些連他都看不見的影子。

當他倒下，我們所摯愛的三人便將重獲自由。

My dear Miko Takeshi,

Today is your thirteenth birthday. I have been waiting many years to write this letter — for I have always known this morning would come.

As the sun rose on Hong Kong, the city below it woke like any other — the Star Ferry crossing the harbor, the tea houses filling, the Muggles going about their day. But beneath these lights, our oldest darkness has stirred again.

This time, he did not return to England. He chose here — a city he believes the light has long forgotten.

And he has done what I have feared, for many years, that he would do.

He has taken Harry. He has taken Ron. He has taken Hermione.

The three who once stood against him in the woods of Britain are now held somewhere beneath this harbor's lights. He has bound their magic. They cannot reach him. The only ones who can walk where he hides are those whose names he does not know — those he cannot see coming.

The Order has searched the world for three Muggles with the courage to stand in their place tonight. The Order has chosen you, Michael, and Andrea.

Find the four sacred places of this city. At the first — Cat Street — each of you must gather what calls to you. A stone. A coin. A trinket from the stalls. These will be your three weapons against him. The other three places are gates, not markets — speak the seal at each and pass through. Trust the two who walk beside you. Trust the city itself.

When the eighth hour rings across the harbor and the lights begin to dance, you must be ready. That is the moment he is weakest. That is the moment the dark one can fall.

And when he falls, the three we love will be free.

I have always known you would come.

With every belief,
A. P. W. B. Dumbledore
